How does the 2ml trial vial guarantee work?
Every 50ml flacon ships with an identical 2ml vial. Wear it on your pulse points for up to 7 days. If the full flacon remains sealed, we refund it in full — no questions, no restocking fee.
How long until my order ships?
Extraits are decanted to order and leave the atelier within 48 hours in temperature-stabilized, plastic-free cartons. Delivery is carbon-neutral and fully tracked.
Can I refill my flacon instead of rebuying?
Yes — the titanium neckpiece unscrews. Sealed aluminum replenishment capsules cost 30% less than a new flacon, for the life of your bottle.
Is the quiz really free?
Completely. Four questions, an instant archetype calculation, and no email required. We only ask for your address if you choose to subscribe to the Gazette.
