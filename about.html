The Maison · Grasse — Paris

An Olfactory Study in Structure & Emotion

Épée & Fluide was founded on a single conviction: that a fragrance should not disguise the wearer, but reveal them. Every extrait is composed in our Grasse atelier, then refined in Paris against real skin, in real light.

Our Story

Born in the fields of Grasse, raised in Paris

Our founder, a former architectural restorer, approached perfumery the way she once approached stone: patiently, structurally, and with reverence for the material. In 2016 she traded scaffolding for stills, macerating her first amber accord in a borrowed copper alembic above a Grasse flower farm.

A decade later, the atelier remains deliberately small. Each formula matures for a minimum of sixteen weeks before it is allowed to touch glass. We reject synthetic fixatives, cold-extract our botanicals, and test every batch on the wrists of our own team — because a scent that behaves beautifully on blotter paper can still betray its wearer at noon.

The name is our philosophy: épée, the precise blade of structure; fluide, the warmth that structure exists to hold.

Biochemical Dialogue

The Science of Self & Olfaction

Unlike synthetic commercial perfumery designed to mask, our non-linear extracts actively react to body temperature, skin pH, and natural sebum.

Phase I · Kinetics

Thermal Metamorphosis

Each formula uses cold-extracted natural botanicals with unique boiling thresholds. As your pulse points heat from morning movement to evening stillness, latent resins awaken sequentially rather than dissipating uniformly.

Evolution: 12-Hour Gradient
Phase II · Chemistry

Lipidic Absorption

Our extracts are solubilized in unrefined organic jojoba and grape fractions that bind directly to your lipidic mantle — no harsh alcohol prickle, just a warm olfactory shadow uniquely yours.

Purity: 0% Synthetic Fixatives
Phase III · Chronobiology

Circadian Harmony

Formulated to synchronize with diurnal shifts: crisp monoterpenes support morning cognitive clarity, transitioning into calming ambers and sacred woods as dusk settles.

Dynamic: Day-to-Dusk Balance
