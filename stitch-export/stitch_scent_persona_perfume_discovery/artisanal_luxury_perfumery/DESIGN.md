## Brand & Style

This design system expresses modern olfactory luxury: intimate, warm, sensorial, and unapologetically tactile. Tailored for discerning, fragrance-literate individuals aged 24–45, the aesthetic bridges heritage niche perfumery and contemporary gallery minimalism. 

Visual interactions should evoke warmth, substance, and slow discovery. Generous negative space, editorial compositions, velvety warm surfaces, and precise metallic accents avoid cold clinical e-commerce tropes in favor of an opulent, physical boutique experience.

### Visual Foundations
- **Warm Editorial Minimalist**: Atmospheric compositions anchored by generous warm negative space, sculptural alignment, and unhurried visual pacing.
- **Sensory Materiality**: Interfaces rely on velvety matte background surfaces, warm amber tints, and hairline brushed champagne borders instead of heavy drop shadows or skeuomorphic textures.
- **Editorial Balance**: High-contrast, expressive serif typography leads storytelling, disciplined by a rational grotesque sans-serif for product metadata and e-commerce transactions.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from raw perfume ingredients: burnt botanical clay, extracted resinous ambers, macerated dried figs, and brushed flacon brass caps, resting over hand-pressed cotton paper.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#A2482B` — Spiced Sienna / Terracotta)**: The primary visual anchor. Used for primary CTA surfaces, high-priority active indicators, and hero brand moments.
- **Secondary (`#D9822B` — Warm Liquid Amber)**: Used for secondary interactive highlights, fragrance concentration badges, olfactory heat scales, and hover states.
- **Tertiary (`#4A192C` — Deep Macerated Fig)**: The deepest chromatic value. Employed for high-contrast headline accents, nocturnal fragrance profiles, dark mood tags, and luxury packaging callouts.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8F5F0` — Velvety Raw Cream)**: The primary ambient background surface. Replaces stark optical white to ensure an intimate, skin-like warmth across the entire viewport.
- **Neutral Surface Elevated (`#EFE9DF` — Alabaster Sand)**: Used for nested container surfaces, card tiers, quiz modules, and input field backgrounds.
- **Neutral Text Primary (`#241B18` — Charred Bark)**: Deep espresso black for razor-sharp legibility and editorial contrast.
- **Neutral Text Secondary (`#6E5D57` — Dried Vetiver)**: Soft earthen brown for metadata, fragrance note descriptions, and volume labels.
- **Accent Metallic (`#C8A97E` — Brushed Champagne Gold)**: Applied strictly to delicate dividing hairlines, selected chip outlines, and olfactory pyramid tier dividers.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between historic French editorial gravitas and modern Scandinavian utility.

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`)**: Used for brand statements, fragrance titles, accords, and discovery moments. Italicize selected emotive adjectives in narrative passages to create rhythm.
- **Functional Body & Interface (`Hanken Grotesk`)**: Ensures exceptional clarity in ingredient lists, batch numbers, olfactory pyramid labels, and navigational UI.
- **Label Caps Style**: Apply uppercase treatment paired with expanded letter-spacing (`0.12em`) exclusively for mood badges, concentration indicators (e.g., *EXTRAIT DE PARFUM*), and navigational tab titles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an art catalog: deliberate horizontal pauses, asymmetrical balance, and expansive breathing room around flacon photography.

### Grid & Structure
- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: 12-column fluid grid, `3.5rem` outer margin, `1.5rem` column gutters. Maximum content bounds capped at 1440px to preserve editorial tension.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column fluid grid, `2rem` outer margin, `1.25rem` column gutters.
- **Mobile (Below 768px)**: 4-column fluid grid, `1.25rem` outer margin, `1rem` column gutters. Product listings transition from multi-column grids to staggered full-bleed editorial cards.

### Spacing Cadence
- Use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for tight micro-alignments such as mood badges, price tags, and note pill interiors.
- Use `space-md` for standard form inputs, list items, and interactive chip groupings.
- Use `space-lg` to separate fragrance note tiers within the interactive pyramid and within modular product card interiors.
- Use `space-xl` between major narrative sections, discovery steps, and perfume character profiles.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid breaking the warm materiality of artisanal physical vessels, this design system rejects generic synthetic black shadows. Depth is achieved via warm-tinted atmospheric diffusion, tonal surface nesting, and delicate metallic framing.

### Tonal Stratification
- **Canvas Base**: `#F8F5F0` (Velvety Raw Cream) acts as the foundation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Quiz Containers)**: Elevated onto `#EFE9DF` (Alabaster Sand) with a crisp 1px border colored `#E5DDD1`.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Fragrance Drawers & Sticky Carts)**: Surface `#FFFFFF` layered above warm tones, cushioned by an amber-tinted ambient glow: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(74, 25, 44, 0.08)`.
- **Hairline Framing**: Critical modules (such as the Fragrance Pyramid and Accord Gauges) utilize a 1px border in `#C8A97E` (Brushed Champagne) with 40% opacity to emulate jewelry-grade gold leaf inlay.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a soft, architectural shape profile (`roundedness: 1`). Corners are gently eased (`0.25rem` / 4px base, `0.5rem` for cards, `0.75rem` for modals), echoing the chamfered glass shoulders and heavy bases of cut-crystal flacons. Fully pill-shaped geometries are strictly reserved for mood-tag badges and sensory filter chips to mimic rounded river stones or natural raw botanicals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Rich Terracotta (`#A2482B`) fill with stark cream text (`#F8F5F0`). Slight hover elevation shifts background to Deep Fig (`#4A192C`) with a 200ms ease. No drop shadow. Height: 48px, horizontal padding: `space-lg`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button**: Transparent fill with a 1.5px border in Brushed Champagne (`#C8A97E`) and Neutral Primary text (`#241B18`). On hover, fills with `#EFE9DF`.
- **Tertiary Link**: Underlined text in `#A2482B` with 2px underline offset and uppercase `label-caps` typography.

### Mood-Tag Badges & Chips
- **Mood Badges**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded with `space-xs` vertically and `space-sm` horizontally. Background set to neutral cream tint with a fine border in Warm Amber (`#D9822B`). Typography: `label-caps`.
- **Filter Chips**: Soft-cornered (`4px`), background `#EFE9DF`. When selected: background shifts to `#4A192C` (Deep Fig) with crisp cream text.

### Product Cards
- Contained within `#EFE9DF` surfaces with a 1px border in `#E5DDD1`.
- Top area houses a generous 4:5 ratio image container against a soft radial gradient evoking amber diffused light.
- Bottom area stacks: Mood-Tag badge, Fragrance Name in `headline-sm` (`Playfair Display`), olfactory family subline in `label-caps`, and direct discovery trigger in `body-sm`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Height: 48px. Flat surface in `#F8F5F0` bounded by a 1px border in `#D8CEBF`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#A2482B` (Terracotta) with an outer 2px highlight in `#A2482B` at 15% opacity. Typography inside uses `body-md` in Charred Bark.

### Interactive Fragrance Note Pyramid
- A vertical tripartite component (Top Notes, Heart Notes, Base Notes) separated by hairline brushed champagne dividers.
- **Top Tier (Head)**: Airy layout with translucent amber pills highlighting volatile notes (e.g., Bergamot, Pink Pepper).
- **Heart Tier (Soul)**: Warm terracotta-tinted containers emphasizing floral and spice absolutes.
- **Base Tier (Foundation)**: Deep fig and charred bark grounded blocks indicating heavy resins, oud, and sandalwood with high visual weight.

### Sensory Quiz Containers
- Contained in an expansive framed card (`#EFE9DF`) with generous `space-xl` padding.
- Interactive question selectors feature high-contrast photography paired with an ambient tinted overlay that deepens from warm amber to terracotta upon hover and selection.
